FCL 2026 Wellness Challenge — Registration Guide
==================================================

Open the App
Open your phone's web browser and go to:
https://wellness-challenge.vercel.app

Add to Home Screen (Recommended)
iPhone: Tap the Share button (square with arrow) → Scroll down → Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Android: Tap the menu (three dots) → Tap "Add to Home Screen"
This creates an app icon on your phone for easy one-tap access.

Create Your Account
1. Enter your email address
2. Create a password (at least 6 characters)
3. Tap "Sign up"
4. You're signed in — no confirmation email needed

Complete Your Profile
1. Enter your first and last name
2. Optionally choose a username (shown on leaderboards instead of your name)
3. Select your business unit from the list
4. Answer whether you're located at CRC
5. Select your age range
6. Tap "Continue"

Join or Create a Team (Optional)
- Create a new team: Enter a team name and tap "Create team"
- Join an existing team: Tap "Join an existing team" and pick one from the list
- Skip for now: You can join a team later

You're Registered!
You'll see a confirmation screen with a countdown to October 5.
Share the app link with coworkers and invite them to your team.

Start Logging — October 5
Come back on October 5 to start earning points:
✓ Log wellness activities — every minute counts (1 point per minute)
✓ Complete one wellness check-in per week (+20 points)
✓ Earn points, win prizes, and compete with your team

Need help? Use the feedback button in the app or contact Patrick.
