The FCL Wellness Challenge is back!

Register here: https://fclwellnesschallengeapp.ca

iPhone: Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen
Android: Tap the menu (three dots), then Add to Home Screen

1. Open the app on your phone

5. You're registered!

Once you're in, you'll see a countdown to October 5. Share the link with your coworkers and get your team ready.

What you're playing for:
Weekly prize draws: two $75 Co-op gift cards each week
Grand prize: two winners receive a paid day off
Team lunch for the top team, plus one random team draw

Registration takes two minutes. See you on the start line!
